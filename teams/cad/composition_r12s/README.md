All other limits hold for all three (`report_p1.json` `limits`, all true): shelves S1_UL 158x182 / S2_UR 194x182 / S3_LL 188x158 flat tops, clear above 300 mm; envelope 914 (hard clip); core >= 50.8 mm, cover over rod >= 20.2 mm (spec 22 / 5); pad dia 70, 38 mm to the plane + 14 mm embedded (52 mm total along the axis, spec >= 30), flat face + D-flat spigot socket + 44 mm rod bore as r11; rod 10 mm steel (unchanged spec), embed 82 mm, root block / plate 100% solid (measured 1.0). Gap = ball's lowest back point above the flesh peak under it (114 mm, r11 definition): S1 60, S2 66, S3 80 mm; the closest solid of the rest of the piece (the tallest patch eye) is 17 / 27 / 40 mm from the ball.

**Mass basis.** Mask part modelled as a printed hollow shell: wall 2.0 mm (area from marching cubes of the ball region x 2.0 mm), 10% infill in the interior, PLA 1.24 g/cm3, +5% finish. Pad cylinder inside the ball, the boss outside it and all grip tendrils are counted 100% solid (S2: 92.7 + 168.8 cm3 of 1,976 cm3 total). Volume comes from the voxel model; the exported `build/*_maskpart.stl` is marching cubes of the same voxels so its trimesh volume equals the voxel volume (not an independent check; no final-CAD mesh exists). A solid S2 ball would be 2.45 kg, so the shell/infill assumption is load-bearing: at 15% infill S2 is 0.82 kg (fails). 

**Features.** Carved into the curved front: two vertical ovals (0.078 x 0.176 D) and a thin tapering arc groove (0.046 D wide at the middle, ~0.5x at the ends), recess depth ~0.052 D (9 mm at S2), floor follows the surface, walls along w, edges perturbed (weathered). No nose, no teeth, no applied parts. Bone palette as r11 (the mask is still the brightest value in the front view, as in r11 H). Grip: six cupping tendrils (r 9 -> 3.8) from the pad over the back and sides, tips hooking over the equator onto the front rim (clear of the face); the rod, pad and keyed spigot carry the load (tendrils have no structural credit, S6).

**Arm root moved.** r11 H exit (0, 168) gave 0-4.5 mm free-span clearance against the patch swelling with the ball (r11 H: 6.3), so the root search (radius 48 mm, requires >= 7 mm by the build-time test, measured 18-28 mm) landed at exit (-24, 192, z 106), rod 6-7 deg off the ball's seat normal (vs 25 deg off the wall normal in r11). Consequence: the arm rises under the ball nearly along the wall normal; the 6 root tendrils and the arm plate moved with it, so the "rest" differs from r11 H by 81.6 cm3 (all at the arm root; trunk, maw, shelves, cascade, patch, eyes identical by construction, the same code and seeds).

**Pick: S2 (oblate, depth 0.6 D, 180 x 108 mm).** From the front it is the only one that reads as a form rather than a face-sized disc (S3, 200 x 90, shades nearly like the flat H mask) or a small sticker-sized ball (S1, 120 mm, face half of H's, lost against the eye patch); in the three-quarter and side views the dome and the cupping tendrils read as an object held up, and the carved ovals/groove curve with the surface in the raking close-up. It survives the relief cap (ball front 288, max 286, 14 mm under the cap) while leaving 66 mm of air under its lowest point and 27 mm to the nearest eye so the patch shows from the side, and its mass 0.744 kg sits under 0.8 with 2 mm / 10% (S3 0.774 is within 3% of the cap). Weak points: S2 is 0.8 kg-class only with the 10% infill assumption, and its smiley (D 180) is 10% smaller than H's disc.

## 2. Print orientation of the ball (Structural s.10 N4)
The mask no longer prints back-down (pad face stands 24-38 mm behind the back, tendrils wrap it). Intended: two halves split on the equatorial plane (w = 0) so the carved face is one intact piece. Front half prints cut-face down, dome up, with the carved face last on top (no supports on the face; recess walls vertical, floor within 0.2 mm layers). Back half prints cut-face down with the pad boss and tendrils up; the six tendril tips that hook past the equator (phi up to 106 deg, <= ~10 mm) overhang the split and need supports or must be cut off and bonded on. Lap joint on the split, bonded. Largest part: 180 mm dia, 54 mm per half (S2): fits a 256 mm bed. Slicer: 100% infill modifier over the pad cylinder (r >= 35 mm, 52 mm along the rod) as for the disc. Not designed: split joint, vent hole, the tendril-tip split.

## 4. Known issues (Phase 1 and 2)
- Phase 2 arms are not the r12 arms (root and boss searched again, see s.5): C6 exit x = 82 and C10 exit (112, 110) in particular.
- No FEA, no bench test; 2 mm voxels: bore, D-flat and the 2 mm wall are nominal. The shell/infill figure is an assumption, not a sliced mass.
- Cap margins: ball front 288-294 vs 300; do not add tilt or gap without shrinking D.
- Arm root differs from H (above). The arm is now nearly normal to the wall at the exit, 82 mm into a block under the head, 19 mm arm-part z-range lower than r11 (z 2-194 vs 0-209).
- Eye-recess depth 7-9 mm at 2 mm voxels: groove width 4-8 mm is 2-4 voxels; edges are noisy by design, not by resolution alone.
- The ball is the brightest value in the front render (as r11's disc was); not re-judged against DESIGN_PRINCIPLES checklist or the composition gate (not run for r12s).
- The trimesh warning "invalid value in divide" comes from a degenerate sliver body in the arm part STL, as in r11/r12.

**What changed besides the mask (not variation axes, but the arm differs from r12):**
- Boss position on the ball's back is searched (12 directions x 3 radii) so the rod meets the seat within 35 deg; the root search radius is 70 mm around the r12 preference (r12: 55). Exits therefore moved: C1 (+18, 171), C2 (14, 212), C3 (6, 230), C4 (18, 242), C6 (82, 165), C8 (60, 286), C9 (-54, 175), C10 (112, 110). The patch / mask centre, holder side and pose are unchanged; the holding tentacle's path is not (e.g. C10's exit sits far right, C6's right of the head). Compare with the r12 contact sheet before reading these as the same concepts.
- Gap 66 mm for all (r12 disc: 85); the oblate ball is 108 mm deep, so the front is 286-298 mm. C2, C3, C6 carry max z 298 and C9 298 (2 mm under the cap, same as the r12 discs 294-298 within a few mm): any larger ball, more yaw or more gap fails these four.
- r12 numbers worth comparing: r12 arm-part z 266 (C2) and 250 (C6) -> 206 and 216 with the ball; both under the 256 mm bed height (N3). All eight arm parts are 192-216 mm.
- Thread concepts C3, C6, C10: the left eye is bored through the ball (0.115 x 0.21 D, 21 x 38 mm at S2) and the tentacle (r 6 -> 4.2) passes it. Wall between the bore and the pad / spigot zone: C3 15.4, C6 24.2, C10 23.4 mm (>= 3 required). It is cosmetic only (the rod and pad hold the ball). In the renders it reads weakly: a small tentacle lip at the left eye, not a clear thread over the face; the hole itself reads as a larger eye. If it does not read for Evan, drop it back to a recessed eye (a one-line change in `ball.py`).
- Coil concepts C2, C8: a single tentacle spirals 1.15 turns round the equator band (r 9.5 -> 4.5); from the front it appears as a rim ring and in the side views as a belt; C2's holder is on the left and yaw 33 deg turns the face away, so the wrap is the more visible element there.
- C4 (O P F M X E LL T U) and C10 (R P E M L U UR T R) are the 'partly lowered' poses; the ball overlaps the maw top / patch in the front view, ball-to-rest minimum 48.7 / 42.2 mm.

**Pass / fail:** all eight PASS every numeric limit. None needed a variation-axis change. Not re-run: composition gate and DESIGN_PRINCIPLES checklist (r12 values do not carry over: the mask silhouette and root path changed). C4's r12 gate failure (3/7) and C8's (5/7) come from trunk width and limb count and are untouched by the ball.
