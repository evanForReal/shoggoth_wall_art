## 2. Unintended changes / blunt notes (read these before reading the cards)
- **A0 vs A:** besides the crown going, the head now reads as a bare rounded cap with a thick brow ring. At thumbnail size it is a quiet dome with a bright smiley: closer to r09 S5's head than to A's. It is the control, so every card shares it. The gate's `limb_count` now passes (8; A had 11 with the tines).
- **T1:** the arm webs are re-strung on the new radii, so they are not identical to A0's: the UL web nearly vanishes on the 30 mm arm. The 30 mm arm under the S1_UL plate is a 12-20 mm tube; the plate is carried by the fingers only (structural flag for CAD). Silhouette differs from A0 by 22,300 mm2 (arms).
- **T2:** the whip root is inside S2_UR, so S2_UR's arm and web are unchanged. Whip at x 444 touches the envelope edge (hard-clipped flat there, a few mm). It is the only study with a longer element on the right than A's H5.
- **T3:** suckers sit on a braided surface (placed on the actual surface z); ring height 0.7 r, pit 0.55 r. 4-7 are visible per arm. Whole-footprint test finds 2 mm of ring above the S3_LL plate plane at the arm/plate junction, outside the flat patch.
- **E1:** first placement (right of the mask) cut into S2_UR's flatten box and was thrown away; eye is left of the mask. The swelling widens the head silhouette by 8,300 mm2; the eye sits on a lump at the head's upper left that reads as an ear/horn bump, and the S1_UL arm passes just below it. Bulge enters the S1_UL footprint box by <= 22 mm above the plane (outside the flat patch, clear 300 over the flat).
- **E2:** swelling adds 5,200 mm2 of silhouette. Eyes overlay two ribs. Pupils < 45 mm are painted only (no carve); clouded = painted pale. Checklist #4 (mask the single brightest) fails: pale eyes approach the mask value.
- **M1/M2:** only the maw changes (solid diff confined to x -66..98, y -174..144, z 34..106; silhouette identical). Maw luminance 7.6% -> 17.6% (M1) / 25.4% (M2) by pale teeth; checklist #4 fails for M2. Teeth project horizontally 10-45 mm over the cavity: overhangs on a face-up print, rotation/support decision needed before CAD. M2's 250 teeth of 6-8 mm base are below the 9 mm strand floor used elsewhere.
- **D1/D2:** D1 also lowers the gate limb_count (6 vs 8; still passes) and fill (0.374 vs 0.405). D2 is only mildly denser than A0 (fill 0.426 vs 0.405): A0 is already r08c "b", and "c" differs from it by little once the crown is gone. D2's hole numbers also change the 3 wings, not only the new sheets (coupled, not isolated).
- **O1/O2 are rougher than the rest.** (a) The arm wings were dropped: they were anchored on a vertical trunk flank and produced slivers on a diagonal trunk; webs = strand-to-strand sheets only (3 / 2 vs 7 in A0). (b) Cascade strands hand-authored, fewer (6 / 5 vs 8). (c) Trunk half-width 138-150 (A: 124-132; r07 G2a lean 150, G4a 150) so the maw 150 x 316 fits; maw tips still spill past the thin trunk ends and show wall. (d) O1 ML shelf lowered from r07's y -60 to -110: at -60 the UL plate underside sat 298 mm above it and the 300 mm clear failed. (e) Ribs routed by spine-normal offset around the maw; weak read. (f) Trunk is a smooth fat diagonal slab (gate torso width 0.53 / 0.59, band <= 0.598); IoU vs c01 0.41 / 0.37 (info). (g) O1 left side reads as a large wedge behind the maw.
- **O3:** the tilt is 15 deg as asked; the smiley's tilt is subtle at thumbnail size. The cradle bulge was not requested (needed for attachment). Mask rim overhangs the head by ~46 mm left and top. Silhouette +7,000 mm2.
- **No stance:** judged by eye, not measured. A0-family: H1 and H5 remain two long diverging strands (inherited from A, r08c README s.3); T2 adds a third on the right. O1/O2 bodies are diagonal, no pair of legs, but their lower cascades also splay.
- All 13: envelope hard-clipped (bbox x/y +-456, z <= 268); headroom measure treats space above the envelope as empty (UL top 285 leaves only 172 mm to the envelope edge, UR 307, O1 UL 127; the 300 mm clear is read as "no solid above the plane over the flat patch").
